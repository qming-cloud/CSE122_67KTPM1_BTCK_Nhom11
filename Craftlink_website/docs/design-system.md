## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, humanistic, and dignified digital identity for a fair trade social enterprise and artisan e-commerce platform. It balances artisanal Vietnamese cultural heritage with contemporary, sustainable commerce aesthetics.

The visual style blends **Tactile Organic Modernism** with an **Editorial Heritage** feel:
- **Atmosphere:** Earthy, slow-crafted, respectful, and grounded. It rejects the hyper-gloss and clinical slickness of typical mass-market e-commerce in favor of tactile surfaces, natural dyes, and woven textures.
- **Audience:** Discerning domestic and international patrons of authentic handicrafts, cultural advocates, interior connoisseurs, and conscious consumers prioritizing WFTO ethical origins.
- **Visual Expression:** Generous breathing room, subtle handmade geometry, soft stone-washed surfaces, delicate tactile borders, and rich mineral accents evocative of Northwestern mountainous soil, indigo vats, and earthenware kilns.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural earth and botanical dyes, structured around the "Moss & Clay" landscape:

- **Primary (`#2A3B32` - Deep Forest / Xanh rêu rừng sâu):** Used for foundational typographic hierarchy, primary navigation bars, main brand marks, and high-emphasis structure. Evokes deep moss and highland forest canopies.
- **Secondary / Accent CTA (`#B85B3A` - Earthen Red / Đỏ đất nung):** The action engine of the interface. Applied to interactive purchase paths, urgent status markers, active cart states, and primary CTAs. Evokes terracotta, baked clay, and mountain hearths.
- **Tertiary / Warm Ochre (`#D4A359` - Tây Bắc Ochre / Vàng thổ hoàng):** Highlights craftsmanship certifications, artisan origin callouts, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral / Canvas Canvas (`#F4F3EE` - Soft Oat / Yến mạch dịu):** The global viewport background. Emulates raw handmade paper and untreated linen.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Split between `#FFFFFF` (pure daylight elevation for high-contrast product staging) and `#FAF9F5` (subtle off-white containers for contextual storytelling).
- **Muted Text (`#5A6558`):** Organic mineral gray-green for secondary specifications, metadata, captions, and deactivated controls.
- **Dividers & Borders (`#E5E0D8` - Pebble / Đá cội):** Natural stone gray for 1px tactile borders and subtle rule dividers.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic craft literature and hyper-legible contemporary commerce:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys narrative prestige, artisanal heritage, and respect for Vietnamese cultural traditions. Used for product titles, community story headlines, and hero manifestos. Features classical proportions with warm ligatures.
- **Functional Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a friendly, clear, and modern reading experience for technical dimensions, shipping timelines, localized pricing (VND / USD), origin specifications, and data dense product options.
- **Cultural Transliteration & Vietnamese Diacritics:** Both typefaces ensure optimal rendering and vertical balance for complex Vietnamese accent marks (hỏi, ngã, nặng, sắc, huyền) without clipping or awkward line jump artifacts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in an intentional rhythm reflecting the pacing of slow craft:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max content boundary of 1320px), shifting to an 8-column layout on tablet (640px to 1024px), and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports (<640px).
- **Artisan Product Framing:** Wide margins on desktop (`margin-lg: 4rem`) mimic the matting of museum pieces or gallery catalogs, granting textiles and pottery space to breathe without crowding.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature stories and community spotlights break grid symmetry with 7/5 or 8/4 column pairings, blending editorial portraits of craftswomen directly alongside curated wares.
- **Rhythmic Densities:** Product grids maintain comfortable gaps (`space-lg: 1.5rem` to `gutter: 1.5rem`) allowing product tags and artisan markers to exist harmoniously without visual friction.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids high-gloss synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile organic depth**, low-contrast structural outlines, and natural warmth:

- **Base Elevation (Surface 0):** The global canvas `#F4F3EE` (Soft Oat) rests at absolute ground level.
- **Raised Elevation (Surface 1):** Product cards and content panels sit on `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF9F5`, defined primarily by a delicate 1px solid border (`#E5E0D8 - Pebble`). Ambient depth is subtly added via an earthy, hyper-diffused drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(42, 59, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Drawers (Surface 2):** Flyout shopping carts and quick-view panels utilize a slightly deeper ambient wash: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(42, 59, 50, 0.08)`, combined with the signature 1px `#E5E0D8` rim border.
- **No Cold Gradients:** Blurs and overlays carry an ultra-light tint of `#F4F3EE` at 85% opacity, simulating the soft light filtering through handwoven bamboo blinds.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces **Roundedness Level 2 (8px - 16px)** to reflect organic pottery, hand-turned timber, and smooth river pebbles:

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Standard for form inputs, interactive badges, filter chips, secondary buttons, and contextual dropdowns.
- **Container Radius (16px / `1rem`):** Applied to product cards, testimonial enclosures, narrative image frames, and cart preview panels.
- **Pill Exception (Full Round):** Preserved exclusively for micro-status indicators, Fair Trade WFTO verification tags, and ethnic community heritage stamps (e.g., *H'Mông*, *Thái*, *Chăm*, *Dao*).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Solid `#B85B3A` (Earthen Red) fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Border-radius of 8px. Hover state shifts to `#9F4E31` with a subtle transform lift of -1px. Focus ring uses 2px solid `#B85B3A` offset by 2px with `#F4F3EE`.
- **Secondary Brand Button:** Solid `#2A3B32` (Deep Forest) with `#FAF9F5` typography. For high-importance non-purchase actions (e.g., "Meet the Artisan", "Read Origin Story").
- **Tertiary / Outline:** 1px solid `#2A3B32` border, transparent background, text in `#2A3B32`. Active and hover states fill with `#FAF9F5`.
- **Text Button:** Underlined with a natural 1.5px stroke of `#B85B3A` offset by 4px, transitioning smoothly on hover.

### Badges & Provenance Tags
- **Ethnic Origin Badge:** Solid `#FAF9F5` container, 1px solid `#E5E0D8`, text in `#2A3B32` (`label-sm`), featuring a subtle leading dot tinted with `#D4A359` (Ochre). Shapes are fully rounded pills.
- **Fair Trade WFTO Seal:** `#2A3B32` background with `#FAF9F5` crisp text and an organic leaf/guarantee glyph, signaling ethical trade compliance.

### Product Cards
- **Construction:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E5E0D8`, 16px border-radius.
- **Image Presentation:** 4:5 aspect ratio image framed with a gentle `#F4F3EE` inner background. Hover reveals an authentic secondary lifestyle image or artisan making-process snapshot.
- **Content Stacking:** Origin tag (e.g., *Mộc Châu / H'Mông*) at top, followed by `headline-sm` product title in `#2A3B32`, finishing with price in `#B85B3A` (`title-md` font weight) and muted natural materials metadata in `#5A6558`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Background `#FFFFFF`, height 48px, border 1px solid `#E5E0D8`, border-radius 8px.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to 1.5px solid `#2A3B32` with a subtle outline glow of `rgba(42, 59, 50, 0.1)`. Placeholder text in `#5A6558`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Style:** Handcrafted precision; custom 20px inputs with 1.5px border `#5A6558`. Checked state fills with `#2A3B32` displaying an ivory check or dot, maintaining a grounded tactile look.

### Community / Artisan Impact Card (Specialty Component)
- Horizontal dual-container with warm `#FAF9F5` surface. Left side frames an authentic artisan portrait with 12px radius; right side displays the craftswoman's biography, ethnic group history, village location, and an impact stat (e.g., "Directly supports 14 weaving families in Mai Châu").
